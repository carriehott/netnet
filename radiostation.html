Mobile Solar Radio Station 87.0

Created for the Institute for Public Architecture on Governor's Island, New York in August 2026

This solar powered mobile radio station broadcasts sound files from a USB drive to any FM radio tuned to 87.0 within a range of 50 feet. Designed to work without internet or electricity, two inconsistent resources on Governor's Island, this mobile radio station is powered by a solar panel and a small server programmed to serve files from the USB drive. Originally designed to fit atop a cart made from recycled materials, the solar panel and server can be installed inside, outside, or on another mobile cart.


Are you using the radio station?

You can unplug the USB drive and load it with your own sound files. WAV files are supported, and the station will broadcast them to any FM radio tuned to 87.0 within a range of 50 feet.

The solar panel and server should be carefully placed in an area with plenty of sun to keep the charge.


How was this made?

The solar panel is connected to a solar charge controller, which regulates the charge coming in before sending it off to the battery. All of the components between the charge controller and the battery ensure the power is stable, and prevents overcharge and overdischarge. Pretty much everything you see in the box is an electrical component! Except for the server, which is a raspberry pi microcontroller programmed to be a web server and a low power FM radio station. This radio station is specifically programmed to broadcast from a USB drive so that you can load your own sound files.
